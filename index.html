❓ How to Play

All the rules in short, right here.

🌐
🌐 Language

Tap the 🌐 button in the top bar (or pick a language in ⚙️ Settings) to switch between English and Bahasa Indonesia. Your choice is remembered, so the game opens in that language next time.

🧸
Preschool & Elementary tracks

There are 8 preschool levels (ages 4–6, all picture based) and 20 elementary levels (grades 1–6). Pick a track on the 🗺️ Level Map, or press 🧸 Start Preschool / ▶️ Start Learning on the home screen. Each track keeps its own progress.

🧭
Math Adventure track

The third track is an adventure: 10 chapters travelling from Peaceful Village and the Forbidden Forest all the way to Dragon's Lair. Every chapter has an opening story and word problems that get tougher as you go. The map is an exploration path — a trail links Chapter 1 to Chapter 10, the 📍 marker shows where you are now, locked chapters open one by one, and finished chapters get a ✓. Open it from the 🧭 Adventure tab on the Level Map or the 🧭 Start Adventure button on the home screen. Its progress is separate from preschool and elementary.

1️⃣
Pick a level

Each track gets steadily harder. Finish one level to unlock the next.

2️⃣
Answer questions

Each level has 10 random questions, each with 4 answer choices. Pick the one that is correct.

3️⃣
Watch the clock

Every question counts down. A correct answer before time runs out earns a bigger point bonus. The duration (5–180 seconds) or ∞ Unlimited time can be changed in ⚙️ Settings. Unlimited suits young children who are just starting out.

💛
Hearts / lives

You have 3 hearts. A wrong answer costs 1 heart. When the hearts run out the level ends early.

🔥
Streak (combo)

A run of correct answers raises your score multiplier: 1x → 2x → 3x → 4x.

⭐
Stars

Every level awards up to 3 stars. The more correct answers and the faster you are, the more stars you earn.

💡
Hints

Need a hand? The 💡 Hint button costs a few points but can be used whenever you like. You can hide it under ⚙️ Settings → 💡 Hints (handy for confident kids).

👥
Many players

One device can be shared by several kids. Tap the name at the top right (or 👥 Switch Player) to change. Every player has their own stars, levels, history, and badges, so you can just pick up where you left off.

🏆
Leaderboard

The 📱 This Device tab shows every player on this device. When the game runs through server.js, the 🌐 All Players tab shows the ranking from phones and computers on the same network.
